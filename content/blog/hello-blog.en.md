## What I'm working on

I started out in 2014 as a Rails engineer at an AWS reseller, working on a project for ZyXEL. That was my first time building systems primarily for European users, where I wrote backend code, planned testing, and worked with AWS architecture. Over time, my focus shifted toward front-end and full-stack development, mainly working with React, React Native, and Node.js.

Over the past few years, as AI tools have matured, I've started offloading repetitive tasks to AI agents. For example, collaborating with Claude Code on a real estate platform, or using AI to help generate BDD test plans at a data startup.

Whenever I have remaining AI tokens each month, I like to tinker with side projects in my spare time. It's partly a way to practice keeping sensitive data and privacy local, and partly a chance to refresh my backend chops. Examples include my project [PickleScout](https://github.com/iskWang/PickleScout), or training SDXL LoRAs with A1111 / ComfyUI to generate images. For daily life administration, I leave that to [Hermes Agent](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/).

## What I want to write here

This blog mainly documents three things:

- **Technology and workflows:** Pitfalls encountered during development, architectural tradeoffs, or insightful engineering perspectives from the community worth digging into.
- **Everyday life:** Moments and reflections outside of code that are simply worth remembering.
- **Travel planning:** As an INTP, I'm not particularly fond of meticulous long-term planning, but I love problem-solving. Here I'll share my back-and-forth discussions with AI while sorting out itinerary constraints, along with the actual journeys that take shape.
